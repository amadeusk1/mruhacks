## Pieces

World map board, destination arrows, coordinated café/cinema/arcade/fishing/sled/snowball/basketball/drawing signs, home plaque and queue-spot marker.

## Needed versus optional

Readable activity entrances and direction cues support navigation. A separate large sign at every entrance is optional: reuse existing marquees, notice boards, signposts and the homes arch where sufficient. One frame family with interchangeable icon/text inserts is preferable to eight nearly identical meshes.

## Assembly and accessibility

Use original simple icons plus readable names in the game; do not rely only on color or pictograms. Labels and destinations come from actual world data and should support localization. The map insert should match the playable map, not an outdated concept. Floor queue markers can be decals without raised collision; the illustrated stone surround is optional. Keep signs away from narrow paths, camera sightlines, fishing casts and sports trajectories. Home numbering and queue mechanics are not established merely by these props. A reserved-slot state needs authoritative interaction logic, not just a marker.
