## Pieces

Straight/corner skirting, doorway frame, low room divider, wall panel, corner shelf, ceiling beam, pendant light, wall sconce, radiator, waste bin and floor vent.

## Needed versus optional

Frames and trims fill exposed interior seams; reuse existing wall/door components when already complete. Lights can reuse existing lantern glow materials. Shelf, divider, radiator, bin and vent expand optional décor rather than adding utility systems. No heating or ventilation simulation is implied.

## Placement rules

Skirting and wall panels are shell finishes. Shelves and wall lights use wall placement; pendants and beams use ceiling placement; divider and bin use floor footprints. Shelves expose support slots for separate objects. Avoid hidden duplicate wall faces and z-fighting. Radiator placement must not cover a door or interaction area. Ceiling beams/lights participate in roof-cutaway rules. Dividers should not hide the penguin or block the only exit, and all decorative collision should stay simple. Provide separate emissive lamp inserts and reusable attachment points.
