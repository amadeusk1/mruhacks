# Player Housing — Shape and Interior Concepts

New visual explorations for customizable player houses. Furniture is rearrangeable; these scenes illustrate possibilities rather than mandatory layouts.

## Exterior shapes

![Player Homes - Exterior Variations](concepts/Player%20Homes%20-%20Exterior%20Variations.png)

Snow dome, A-frame cabin, round cottage, treehouse, lighthouse loft, and houseboat provide distinct silhouettes and potential room boundaries. Their dimensions, access routes, and availability are still design choices.

## Interior personalities

![Player Homes - Interior Personalities](concepts/Player%20Homes%20-%20Interior%20Personalities.png)

Cozy loft, botanical nook, gamer den, artist studio, coastal retreat, and starlight room show combinations of furniture and finishes. Players should be able to mix these themes rather than being restricted to a fixed furnished preset.

See [Player Housing - Grid Decoration](Player%20Housing%20-%20Grid%20Decoration.md) for the grid-based decorating direction and [Player Housing - Decoration Prop Catalog](Player%20Housing%20-%20Decoration%20Prop%20Catalog.md) for reusable props.

Concept sheets generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt direction: varied cozy faceted house silhouettes and six distinct rearrangeable interior personalities, matching the existing penguin-island palette and diorama style. These are concept images, not finished game assets.
