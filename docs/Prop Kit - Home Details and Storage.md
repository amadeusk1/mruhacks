Hook rail, oval mirror, entry mat, folded blanket, throw, bookends and vase expand the visual catalog. Existing themed rugs and mirrors may be reused.

## Placement and moving parts

Wall items use wall-grid placement; boxes and chests use floor footprints; small decorations use surface slots. Curtains need an opening envelope, chest lids need hinge clearance, and stackable boxes need a defined support surface. Keep books separate from bookends and fruit separate from bowls where player decoration supports it. Throws need compatible furniture presets; arbitrary cloth simulation is not implied. The illustrated dots are annotations, not mesh components.

Storage props are decorative until a storage gameplay system is explicitly chosen. Mirrors can use a stylized insert; real-time reflections are not required by the concept.
