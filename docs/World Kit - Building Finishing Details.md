## Pieces

Foundation skirt, exterior corner trim, window sill, paired shutters, doorway threshold, roof ridge/end caps, eave trim, chimney cap, porch canopy, separate snow-eave overlay and wall-sign bracket.

## Needed versus optional

End caps and trims are needed only where modular assemblies expose unfinished edges. Reuse the existing building kit's stone base, windows, entry steps, chimney, roof and awning wherever they already solve the seam. Shutters and porch variants are optional polish, not required on every building.

## Assembly rules

Use the same module dimensions and roof pitch as the base architecture. Do not stack trims on existing trim faces and create z-fighting. Thresholds must remain traversable; porch posts must leave the full doorway and approach clear. Shutters use hinge pivots if animated. Roof and snow trim should follow the cutaway visibility group so they disappear together indoors. Keep the chimney opening and smoke emitter unobstructed. Sign faces remain replaceable inserts. Large wall/roof patches shown behind isolated pieces are context, not mandatory fused geometry.
